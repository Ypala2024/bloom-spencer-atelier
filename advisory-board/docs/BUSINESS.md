# Bloom & Spencer: business context for the board

> Shared context every agent reads. Facts the owner hasn't confirmed are marked **[owner to confirm]**.

## Who they are
- Luxury jeweller with a studio at Begumpet, Hyderabad (Telangana, India).
- Known for **lab-grown diamonds** and coloured gemstones: **ruby, emerald, sapphire, Paraiba tourmaline**.
- Pieces: **rings, stud earrings, pendants, bracelets**. Bespoke (made-to-order) design is a core service.
- Collections are named after London places: Regent, Belgravia, Mayfair, Knightsbridge, Kensington, Savile, Chelsea.
- Brand: quiet luxury. Black, white and grey look; colour comes from the stones. No discounts, no "buy now".

## How clients reach them
Walk-ins, referrals, Instagram, WhatsApp, the website, and the Bloom & Spencer Atelier companion app (design-your-own and consultation booking via WhatsApp).

## What the owner wants from this board
1. Better decisions on spending (events, marketing, stock, new collections).
2. Faster, more consistent replies to client enquiries.
3. A second opinion before anything that touches the brand.
