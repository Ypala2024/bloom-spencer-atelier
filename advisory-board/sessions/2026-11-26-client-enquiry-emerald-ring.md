## Step 2: specialists check the brief

**gemologist**
Emerald (Mohs 7.5–8) is fine for daily wear **only with protection**. As a doctor she washes her hands often and may wear gloves, so the halo or a bezel matters, and so does no ultrasonic cleaning. Emeralds are usually oil-treated, which must be disclosed. Strong alternative: an **oval lab-grown diamond with two emerald side stones**. The diamond (Mohs 10) takes the daily wear, and the green is still there.

**master-jeweller**
**Makeable with changes:** use a low-profile halo or bezel so the ring doesn't catch on gloves. Band at least 2 mm for daily wear. Time: about 3–5 weeks from CAD approval [owner to confirm]. A mid-January proposal means **CAD approval by about mid-December**. Care card: remove for heavy work, mild soap only, no ultrasonic.
